## Looks

The app comes in 9 UI styles: Skeuomorphism, Neumorphism, Glassmorphism, Claymorphism,
Brutalism, Maximalism, Liquid Glass, Bento Grid and Spatial UI. Switch in the 🎨 Look panel, or open
one directly from [`styles/`](styles/). After changing `index.html`, run
`node scripts/build-styles.js` to update the style folders.

## Reminders on your phone

- **Android:** open the app in Chrome and tap **Turn on**. For the most reliable reminders, use Chrome's menu → **Add to Home screen** and open it from there.
- **iPhone:** in Safari tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, open the app from the Home Screen, then tap **Turn on**. iPhones only allow notifications from apps added this way.
- Reminders come from the open app. If the app is fully closed, a reminder shows up as soon as you open it again, as long as it's within an hour of the time.
